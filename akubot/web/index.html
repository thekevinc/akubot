Start once from WSL

The worker runs in the background, even after you close this dashboard or terminal.

cd ~/workspace/akubot
python3 training/run_full.py start

After a Docker restart, computer restart, or crash, recover with:

cd ~/workspace/akubot
python3 training/run_full.py resume

The dashboard can pause and resume a running worker. Recovery after it exits uses the WSL command above. Checkpoints retain the optimizer, message order, and random state. An abrupt shutdown loses only work since the latest complete checkpoint.

Before starting

Allow roughly 18–24 hours for one pass, plus preparation and final validation. Training needs around 19 GB of free GPU memory. Pause Discord replies and avoid loading another large model while it runs. The launcher leaves your existing services alone.

Secret and DMs are excluded. Original message strings, including links, are retained. Long messages are limited to 512 tokens during training. This teaches style from your outgoing messages; the archive has no surrounding conversation. Training finishes with a new adapter and merged model, ready for a separate Ollama import.
